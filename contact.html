FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What should I do before my first visit?

Come with a clean face if you can, and bring a list (or photos) of the products you use.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, digital gift cards from $50 are in the shop and arrive by email.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking right outside the studio on Juniper Lane.

What’s your cancellation policy?

48 hours’ notice please. It’s a one-on-one studio, so a no-show is an empty hour.

Do you ship skincare?

Yes, flat $8 shipping or free over $75. Local pickup is always free.

Will my skin purge?

Rarely. If extractions are done, you might see a small spot or two for a day or so.

Is it good for sensitive skin?

Yes, it’s one of our gentlest treatments.
