What is Chemical Peel?

Peels speed up your skin’s natural renewal. The tingle means it’s working, the flaking means it’s working, and week two is when the compliments start.


We always prep your skin for two weeks first, which means better results and fewer surprises.


Who it’s for

  • Sun spots and melasma
  • Acne and post-acne marks
  • Uneven texture
  • Early fine lines

What to expect

  1. Two weeks of home prep
  2. Peel applied in thin layers
  3. Soothing mask and SPF
  4. Day 3 text check-in

Questions

Will I have to hide at home?

Not usually. Most people just look a bit dry and flaky for a few days.

When can I peel?

We avoid peels before beach trips and in peak summer for some skin types.
