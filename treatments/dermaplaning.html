What is Dermaplaning?

Dermaplaning uses a sterile blade to gently remove dead skin and fine hair. Your skin looks brighter straight away and your products work better.


Who it’s for

  • Peach fuzz
  • Dull, rough skin
  • Before an event or photos
  • Makeup that won’t sit smoothly

What to expect

  1. Cleanse
  2. Gentle dermaplaning
  3. Hydrating mask
